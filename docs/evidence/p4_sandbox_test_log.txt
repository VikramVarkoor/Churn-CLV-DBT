The new churn/CLV numbers are directionally consistent with what Phase 2 and Phase 3 would

663.29, 565.31, 179.04). Both models rank these four segments by recency alone, with no
reversal. This is a weaker check than it looks: recency is strongly correlated with frequency

recency specifically is driving the LightGBM and CLV predictions rather than frequency or

and CLV outputs produced a result with no scrambled or nonsensical row, which is what a
join bug would most likely look like.
